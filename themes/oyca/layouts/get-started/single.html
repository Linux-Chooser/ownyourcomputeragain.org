you've got this

You're about to do something transformative and wonderful.

You're about to take your computer back. Not everyone knows this is possible, but you found your way here, and we're going to make it easy.

STEP 01

Download a small file

You'll download a Linux image. It's about 2–3 GB, like a large app. That's the whole operating system, ready to go.

STEP 02

Put it on a USB stick

A free tool called Balena Etcher makes this painless. Drag, drop, click. That's it. Your USB becomes a portable Linux machine.

STEP 03

Restart and explore

Your computer boots from the USB. Linux runs live, right there. Your hard drive isn't touched. Nothing changes unless you want it to.

STEP 04

Fall in love (optional, but likely)

Browse the web, open files, explore the desktop. This is Linux. It's yours. When you're ready, one button makes it permanent.
