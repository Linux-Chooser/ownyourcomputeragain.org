# Contributing to Own Your Computer Again

Thank you for your interest in helping people reclaim ownership of their computers. This project exists because of contributors like you.

## Our Mission

We exist to help people worldwide reclaim ownership of their computers through education, advocacy, and community.

## How to Contribute

### Content

The most impactful contributions are content that helps real people make the switch to Linux:

- **Getting-started guides** for specific audiences (students, retirees, small business owners, etc.)
- **Migration walkthroughs** with step-by-step instructions and screenshots
- **Distro comparisons** that help people choose without overwhelming them
- **Myth-busting articles** that address common fears about switching
- **Translations** to reach non-English speakers worldwide

### Design and Development

- Bug fixes and accessibility improvements
- Responsive design testing across devices and browsers
- Performance optimization
- New page templates following the existing design system

### Reporting Issues

Open an issue if you find:
- Broken links or outdated information
- Accessibility barriers
- Inaccurate claims or statistics
- Anything that would confuse a newcomer

## Guidelines

### Voice and Tone

This site talks to people who have never used Linux. Write for them, not for us.

- **Be welcoming.** Never condescending, never gatekeeping.
- **Be honest.** If something has a rough edge, say so. Trust is earned.
- **Be specific.** "Click the blue button" beats "navigate to the appropriate interface element."
- **Be brief.** Respect people's time. Say it once, say it clearly.

### Technical Standards

- All content is written in Markdown with YAML front matter
- The site is built with Hugo (v0.155.2, extended)
- Styles use SCSS following the existing design system (see `themes/oyca/assets/scss/`)
- All pages must be responsive and accessible (WCAG AA minimum)

### Submitting Changes

1. Fork the repository
2. Create a branch with a descriptive name (`add-mint-guide`, `fix-broken-link`)
3. Make your changes
4. Test locally with `./start_webserver.sh`
5. Submit a pull request with a clear description of what you changed and why

### What We Won't Accept

- Content that disparages other operating systems or their users
- Marketing or promotional material for specific companies
- Content that requires technical knowledge to understand (we explain everything)
- Anything that compromises user privacy

## Code of Conduct

Be kind. Be helpful. Remember that every expert was once a beginner. We are building a community that welcomes people in, not one that tests them at the door.

## Questions?

Open an issue with the label `question` and we will get back to you.
